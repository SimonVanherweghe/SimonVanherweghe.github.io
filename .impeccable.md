# Impeccable

Design context for **donebysimon.be** — Simon Vanherweghe's personal maker's blog.
This file guides all design work on the site. Read it before building or changing any UI.

## Design Context

### Users

- **Primary**: casual visitors curious about what Simon makes — fellow makers and developers, students (Simon lectures in web development and creative code), and people who received one of his physical projects (plotter cards, bots, crafts) and came to see the story behind it.
- **Secondary**: people sizing Simon up professionally — collaborators, event organisers, the curious-after-a-talk.
- **Context of use**: browsing casually — during a break, from a link in a class/talk/social post. Not task-driven; they came to look, read a story, and enjoy.
- **Job to be done**: quickly get a feel for who Simon is and what he makes; enjoy one project's story end to end; be delighted enough to open another.

### Brand Personality

- **Voice** (unchanged): a first-person maker's log — humble, playful, self-deprecating ("Mostly experiments, occasionally finished."). Shows the pile of failed tests, not only the glossy shot. English posts, a Dutch archive.
- **Visual register**: constructed, confident, calm. A modernist composition at the top of the page, quiet space below it. NOT beige/cream, NOT retro, NOT craft-textured, NOT childish, and not a visible quote of any artwork.

### Aesthetic Direction: "Composition" (signed off 2026-10-31)

Inspired by how Sophie Taeuber-Arp divided a surface, with Vera Molnar and Georg Nees as influences on method only. The influence is structural: nothing quotes their pictures.

- **Structure**: the header, hero, post header, pagination and footer are one 12-column grid of abutting rectangular planes, no gutters, separated by 2px rules. Photos are planes too. Circles appear only inside a plane, as punctuation.
- **Two speeds**: the planes are the bold part. The post gallery and reading text are deliberately quiet: no rules, dots, labels or colour, only space. Rejected for the listing: mirrored rows, per-project circles, colour keys, contact-sheet tiles with lines.
- **Type**: **Jost** (display: wordmark, titles, nav, labels; weight 600, set lowercase with CSS `text-transform`, nodding to the magazine *Plastique*) and **Albert Sans** (body and metadata). Self-hosted via `@fontsource-variable`. Code uses the system monospace stack. Bricolage Grotesque, Hanken Grotesk and Fragment Mono are gone.
- **Headline leading**: 1.04 at large sizes, never tighter than 1.0: Jost's `g` hits the dot of an `i` on the next line at 0.92.
- **Palette** (hex, `src/css/tokens.css`). Pure white ground, never beige.

- **Home**: header planes; title `things done` / `by simon` on two lines; hero with red plane, blue plane with white circle, newest post's photo, black "latest" plane, title and description. Below it "all work" and a gallery of every other post: 3:2 photo, date, title, description. Two columns, 56px column gap, 88px row gap; three columns from 1600px; one column below 860px.
- **Post**: planes around title, photo and description; details in a left column (date, tags, author); text in a 720px measure; older/newer as blue and red planes.
- **Width**: the composition runs full width up to 1920px. Reading text stays capped.
- **Mobile**: a 4-column version of the same grid, not a plain stack. Wordmark (3) beside red square (1), nav below; hero planes as a strip of two.
- **Interaction**: quiet. Hover/focus = title underline. No image hover-zoom, no entrance animation.
- **Anti-references**: beige backgrounds, literal Molnár/Schotter scatter, glow, Disney-style play, AI tells (hover-scale, gradient text, glassmorphism, `border-left` accent stripes, uniform icon+heading cards).

### Design Principles

1. **The page is a composition** at the top, abutting planes on one grid, with colour as structure rather than decoration.
2. **Bold planes, quiet content**: never bring planes, rules or colour into the gallery or reading text.
3. **Influence through structure**: borrow how a surface is divided, never how a picture looks.
4. **Legible first**: long-form reading matters; protect measure, contrast, rhythm.
5. **No AI tells**: no beige, hover-zoom, gradient text, glassmorphism or side-stripe borders.
6. **Both themes as equals; accessibility stays in the DNA**: real alt text, visible focus, honored `prefers-reduced-motion`, WCAG-AA in both themes.

### Technical Constraints

- **Stack**: Astro static site → GitHub Pages (push to `main` → `gh-pages` → live). Self-hosted fonts via `@fontsource-variable` only.
- **Foundations**: fluid (Utopia) `--step-*` / `--space-*` scales and legacy-name colour tokens in `src/css/tokens.css`; reading measure `45rem` (720px), composition up to `120rem` (1920px); scoped `<style>` per component. Theme = `prefers-color-scheme` + a persisted manual toggle (`ThemeToggle.astro`), applied via `data-theme` with a no-flash head script.
- **Public repo** — no session links / private trailers in commits.
